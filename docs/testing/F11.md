# F11 test steps: viewing slots

Feature: a member sees the place before renting it, at a time the landlord offered.
- **Visit times**: the ad's landlord side (the poster and the building's caretakers) adds times to a live ad from **My ads**, **Visit times**: a day in the next 14 days, from and until (between 7 am and 10 pm), and how long one visit takes (15 to 60 minutes). The form shows the times it will add (at most 12 at once, 40 coming up per ad). Times cannot overlap, and each time is for one visit.
- **Booking**: a member whose request is shortlisted (or who has an offer) books a free time on the request's page, up to 1 hour before it starts. They then see the address with a map link. They can move the visit to another time or cancel it until it starts. Members who are told about new times: shortlisted members without a visit, once until they open the notice.
- **The landlord side** sees who booked which time, on the **Visit times** page, on the request's page, in the requests list and on the dashboard, and can remove a time that has not started (a member who booked it is told).
- **When a request closes** (declined, withdrawn, expired), its coming visit is freed. Withdrawing a shortlisted request with a visit tells the landlord side. A confirmed tenant keeps their visit.
- **Notifications** (in the app and by email): a visit booked or moved (landlord side), cancelled (the other side), new times to book (the member), and a reminder to both sides 3 hours before the visit (the scheduled job).
- A request with a coming visit, or a visit in the last 14 days, does not close as stale (F10's 14 day rule).

Done when (FR32): the landlord adds times, a shortlisted member books one and moves it, the other member cannot take a booked time, both sides see the visit and get the reminder, and a closed request frees its time.

```sql
-- Query 2: move M3's coming visit to 2 hours from now, as if it was booked a day ago
update public.viewing_slots s
set starts_at = now() + interval '2 hours', ends_at = now() + interval '2 hours 30 minutes',
    booked_at = now() - interval '1 day', reminded_at = null
from public.rental_requests r
where r.id = s.booked_request_id and s.starts_at > now()
  and r.tenant_id = (select id from auth.users where email = 'housemanagerbd+m3@gmail.com')
returning s.id, s.starts_at at time zone 'Asia/Dhaka' as starts_dhaka, s.booked_at;
```

## 13. Privacy (Supabase SQL Editor)
Run each query on its own.

```sql
-- Query 4: visitors and members cannot read or write what is not theirs
-- (expect: false, false, false, false)
select
  has_table_privilege('anon', 'public.viewing_slots', 'select')                  as visitor_reads_times,
  has_table_privilege('authenticated', 'public.viewing_slots', 'insert')         as member_writes_times,
  has_function_privilege('authenticated', 'public.remind_viewings()', 'execute') as member_sends_reminders,
  has_function_privilege('anon', 'public.book_viewing(uuid, uuid)', 'execute')   as visitor_books;
```
