# F10 test steps: managing ads and properties

Feature: posters look after their ads after publishing, owners share a building with caretakers, and members keep a list of saved ads.
- **Edit a live ad**: the same steps as posting (text, costs, tenants, contact number, photos), without the ad going offline. The ad type stays as it is, and a live ad keeps at least 4 photos. If a change sets off an automatic check that an admin has not seen on this ad before (for example a banned word, a phone number in the text, a rent far from the usual, another member's phone number, another member's photo), the ad waits for review again.
- **Pause** and **Resume**: a paused ad keeps its link, its requests and its messages, but is not in search or on the map, takes no new requests, and does not show its phone number to new people.
- **Expiry**: an ad is live for 30 days. The poster gets a reminder 3 days before (in the app and by email) and a notice when it expires. **Renew** gives 30 more days, in the last 7 days or up to 30 days after it expired.
- **Mark as rented**: for a tenant found outside the site. The ad leaves search, the unit becomes rented, and every member still waiting is told "The place is no longer available".
- **Re-list**: a rented or expired ad becomes a new draft with the same details and photos, for the poster to check and send in. **Mark as vacant** on a unit does the same for a unit only.
- **Requests that close**: a request with no step and no message for 14 days closes, and both sides are told. The member can send a new one.
- **Caretakers**: the owner invites a member by email; the member accepts on **My properties** and then posts ads and answers requests for the building. The owner can remove a caretaker, and a caretaker can leave; their ads in the building pass to the owner.
- **Saved ads**: a heart on search results and a **Save** button on an ad's page; **Saved ads** in the seeker menu.
- **Scheduled job**: Supabase Cron runs all the timed steps every 10 minutes (docs/SETUP.md 8.9). For testing, an admin runs it at once from the admin overview.

Done when (FR30, FR31, FR36, FR37): a poster edits, pauses and renews a live ad, marks it rented and re-lists the place with its details and photos filled in; an ad past 30 days expires with a reminder first; an owner adds a caretaker who can then answer the building's requests; and a member saves an ad and finds it under Saved ads.

## 13. Privacy (Supabase SQL Editor)
Run each query on its own.

```sql
-- Query 4: visitors and members cannot read or write what is not theirs
-- (expect: false, false, false, false, false)
select
  has_table_privilege('anon', 'public.saved_listings', 'select')                    as visitor_reads_saved,
  has_table_privilege('authenticated', 'public.saved_listings', 'insert')           as member_writes_saved,
  has_table_privilege('authenticated', 'public.building_invites', 'insert')         as member_writes_invites,
  has_function_privilege('authenticated', 'private.run_scheduled_jobs(boolean)', 'execute') as member_runs_job,
  has_function_privilege('anon', 'public.admin_run_scheduled_jobs()', 'execute')    as visitor_runs_job;
```

```sql
-- Query 5: M2 sees only M2's own saved ads, and no invitations of N's building
-- (expect: two equal numbers, then 0)
select set_config('request.jwt.claims', json_build_object('sub', id, 'role', 'authenticated')::text, true)
from auth.users where email = 'housemanagerbd+m2@gmail.com';
set local role authenticated;
select count(*) as visible_saved, count(*) filter (where user_id = auth.uid()) as own_saved,
       (select count(*) from public.building_invites) as visible_invites
from public.saved_listings;
```

```sql
-- Query 7: as F9's clean-up (tenancies first), plus the test accounts'

   or landlord_id in (select id from auth.users where email like 'housemanagerbd+%@gmail.com');
delete from public.rental_requests
where tenant_id in (select id from auth.users where email like 'housemanagerbd+%@gmail.com');
delete from public.saved_listings
where user_id in (select id from auth.users where email like 'housemanagerbd+%@gmail.com');
delete from public.building_invites
where invited_by in (select id from auth.users where email like 'housemanagerbd+%@gmail.com')
   or email like 'housemanagerbd+%@gmail.com';
delete from public.listings
where posted_by in (select id from auth.users where email like 'housemanagerbd+%@gmail.com');
delete from public.units
where building_id in (select id from public.buildings
  where created_by in (select id from auth.users where email like 'housemanagerbd+%@gmail.com'));
delete from public.buildings
where created_by in (select id from auth.users where email like 'housemanagerbd+%@gmail.com');
delete from public.notifications
```
